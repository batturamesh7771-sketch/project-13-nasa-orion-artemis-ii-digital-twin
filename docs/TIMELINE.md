# Artemis Mission Timeline: Launch to Splashdown

This mission timeline follows the canonical NASA Artemis lunar trajectory profile (representative of Artemis I actual mission execution and Artemis II planned crewed flight).

```
   [T+0:00] Liftoff (KSC LC-39B)
      |
   [T+2:12] Solid Rocket Booster (SRB) Separation
      |
   [T+3:20] Launch Abort System (LAS) Jettison
      |
   [T+8:04] Core Stage MECO & Separation
      |
   [T+18:20] Orion Solar Array Deployment (4 wings)
      |
   [T+1:38:00] Trans-Lunar Injection (TLI) Burn by ICPS (ΔV ≈ 3,050 m/s)
      |
   [T+2:06:00] ICPS Separation & Orion ESM Active Control
      |
   [Flight Day 1-4] Translunar Coast & Mid-Course Corrections (OTC-1, OTC-2)
      |
   [Flight Day 5] Outbound Powered Flyby (OPF) Burn (Closest Lunar Approach ~130 km)
      |
   [Flight Day 6-9] Distant Retrograde Orbit (DRO) / Free-Return Arc (Max Earth Dist ~432,000 km)
      |
   [Flight Day 10] Return Powered Flyby (RPF) Slingshot Burn
      |
   [Flight Day 11-13] Trans-Earth Return Coast & Entry Course Corrections (RTC-1 to RTC-4)
      |
   [T-40 min] Crew Module / Service Module (CM/SM) Separation
      |
   [T-20 min] CM Entry Attitude Slew (Heat Shield Forward)
      |
   [T=0 Entry] Entry Interface (EI) at 122 km (Mach 32 / ~11 km/s)
      |
   [Entry+2 min] Peak Heating (~2,760°C) & Plasma Blackout
      |
   [Entry+4 min] Skip-Entry Lift Maneuver & Descent Phase
      |
   [Entry+7 min] Drogue Parachute Deploy at 24,000 ft (7.3 km)
      |
   [Entry+8 min] Main Parachute Deploy at 9,000 ft (2.7 km)
      |
   [Splashdown] Pacific Ocean Splashdown (~7.8 m/s) & CMUS Uprighting
```
